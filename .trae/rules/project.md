General Standards:
- Maintain clean, consistent, and readable code.
- Use clear naming conventions and logical structure.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Design and Structure:
- Keep components and modules focused on a single responsibility.
- Ensure separation of concerns across the codebase.
- Write code that is easy to extend and maintain.

Error Handling:
- Validate inputs and handle edge cases appropriately.
- Provide clear and actionable error messages.

Performance:
- Minimize redundant operations.
- Handle data and external calls efficiently.

Security:
- Do not expose sensitive or confidential data.
- Ensure proper validation and sanitization of all inputs.

Development Approach:
- Understand requirements before implementation.
- Apply a structured approach to problem-solving.
- Deliver complete and functional outputs.
